## Design Context

### Users
Student founders and builders at University of Birmingham / UoB Dubai. They arrive curious, time-poor, and scanning for a clear next action (join a team / apply to B-Labs).

### Brand Personality
Bold, Swiss-brutal, founder-urgent. Confidence over charm. Red is a signal, not decoration.

### Aesthetic Direction
High-contrast black / white / `#CC0000`. Heavy display type, tight tracking labels, hard edges. Portfolio language (tickers, cohorts, intake) over soft SaaS chrome.
Anti-references: purple gradients, cream+serif terracotta kits, glow stacks, bouncy motion, generic rounded-pill marketing widgets as the hero treatment.

### Design Principles
1. One signature beat per feature — choreograph entrances; don’t sprinkle effects.
2. Motion only on `transform` / `opacity`; respect `prefers-reduced-motion`.
3. Brand first: SEA / B-Labs red and black must remain the hero signal.
4. Every control names the outcome (“Join a startup”), not the system.
5. Mobile and desktop share the same composition; tap targets stay ≥44px.
